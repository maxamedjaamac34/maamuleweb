Maamuleschool

Simple school management for the Horn of Africa.

Coming soon

Maamuleschool helps a school keep classes, attendance, grades, fees, and reports in one calm place. Principals, teachers, students, and parents each see only what is theirs.

Who it is for

Principal

Run the school. People, classes, money, reports, and who is allowed to see what.

Teacher

Take attendance, enter grades, and follow the students in the classes you teach.

Student

See your own grades, attendance, and what you still owe. Nothing about other students.

Parent

Follow your children’s grades, attendance, and fees, and pay only for them.

Everyday school work

Each part opens only for the people who are allowed to use it.

Dashboard

A clear home for each person, with the numbers that matter and the time they were last updated.

Students

Student records stay accurate, searchable, and limited to the right people.

Teachers

See who teaches, and keep each teacher’s classes and students together.

Classes

Daily schedules, subjects, rooms, and who is enrolled.

Attendance

Mark who is present or absent, then review the term without losing a day.

Academics

Classwork, marks, and a simple view of how a student is progressing.

Reports

Term summaries for attendance, grades, fees, and the life of the school.

Notifications

Short updates, sent only to the people who need to hear them.

School money

Fees, costs, and outside support, with a clear balance for each channel.

Fee collection

Student invoices and payments, with a clear paid or still-owed status.

  • Zaad
  • eDahab
  • Bank
  • Cash

Payroll and expenses

Teacher salaries and everyday school costs, grouped so spending is easy to read.

Funding and deposits

Government support and other money coming in, with source, amount, date, and account.

Accounts

Balances by channel, built from fees collected, deposits received, and money paid out.

Running the school

Users and permissions

Create accounts, assign roles, and reset a password when someone is locked out.

Settings

School name, city, country, and level. The principal can update the same details later.

Audit logs

A record of sensitive changes, so leadership can see what happened and when.

Guidance and feedback

Short hints on each screen, and a simple way to say what should be clearer.

Start a school

First-time signup

The principal enters their name, email, phone, and password, then the school’s name, city, country, and level. When setup is done, they are signed in. A school is set up once.
